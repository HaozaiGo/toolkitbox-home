Privacy Policy

Privacy-first tools for everyday developer work.

Last updated: May 22, 2026. ToolkitBox is built around browser-side utilities and minimal data collection.

Data processed by tools

Most ToolkitBox tools process text, files, and generated output locally in your browser. We do not require accounts and we do not intentionally store the content you paste into browser-side tools.

Server-side checks

Some features may need a network request to work, such as SSL certificate checks. In those cases, the requested hostname or URL-like input may be sent to our server or hosting provider only to perform the requested check.

Analytics and advertising

We may use privacy-conscious analytics and, if enabled, advertising services such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Cookies and consent

Essential browser storage may be used to remember local preferences. Advertising or analytics providers may set cookies, measure visits, or personalize ads where allowed. Visitors can control cookies through browser settings and, where presented, consent controls.

What we do not ask for

ToolkitBox does not require a user account for its public tools. We do not ask visitors to submit passwords, payment information, government IDs, or production secrets in order to use the directory pages or guides.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the contact page.
