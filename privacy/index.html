Privacy Policy

Privacy-first tools for everyday developer work.

Content updated: October 20, 2026. ToolkitBox is built around browser-side utilities and minimal data collection.

Data processed by tools

Browser-side tools are intended to transform input on your device, without an account. Local input processing does not mean a page is offline or makes no third-party requests: hosting, analytics, and advertising are separate data flows. Do not enter production secrets or customer records; use synthetic samples and check the specific tool's behavior.

Server-side checks

Some features may need a network request to work, such as SSL certificate checks. In those cases, the requested hostname or URL-like input may be sent to our server or hosting provider only to perform the requested check.

Analytics and technical data

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate page usage and site performance. Hosting and analytics providers may receive limited technical information such as the requested page, browser type, device type, approximate region, and request timing. Analytics is separate from the input transformation. Avoid putting sensitive values in page URLs, which may appear in hosting or analytics records; a local-processing label is not a guarantee about every script or browser extension.

Google advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to ToolkitBox or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites.

Personalized advertising choices

Visitors can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Visitors may also use AboutAds opt-out tools for participating third-party vendors. Google AdSense is the intended advertising provider; this policy will be updated if additional advertising networks are introduced.

Advertising placement

Advertisements, when shown, should be distinguishable from navigation links, tool inputs, copy buttons, download buttons, and generated output. We do not ask visitors to click ads, and we do not intentionally place ads where they can be confused with developer tool controls.

Privacy choices and consent availability

Local browser storage may remember preferences. This page does not load an advertising tag or a consent-manager script. Publication and regional coverage of a certified CMP have not been verified in the AdSense dashboard; we do not claim that a consent dialog is active here.

Reopen consent dialog when available

If a Google consent manager is loaded and callable on the current page, this link asks it to reopen. Otherwise no consent choice is changed.

  • Manage Google advertising preferences. These settings do not replace site-specific consent or block all ads.
  • Use your browser's privacy settings to block cookies or clear site data for toolkitbox.org and any tool subdomains you visited. Clearing data can reset saved preferences; it is not a request to delete server records.
  • Contact ToolkitBox about privacy or unavailable controls. Do not include private tool input.

A privacy link is not a CMP. Applicable consent collection and withdrawal must be configured and tested with the provider before relying on advertising in regions that require them. See Google's CMP requirements.

What we do not ask for

ToolkitBox does not require a user account for its public tools. We do not ask visitors to submit passwords, payment information, government IDs, or production secrets in order to use the directory pages or guides.
