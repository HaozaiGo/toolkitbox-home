Editorial Policy

How ToolkitBox tests tools and maintains guides.

Reviewed September 8, 2026. ToolkitBox is independently maintained by HaozaiGo, the owner of the linked public GitHub projects.

Purpose

ToolkitBox publishes working utilities and practical explanations for common developer tasks. A guide should help a reader complete a task, understand important limits, and avoid exposing production credentials or private records.

Tool testing

Browser tools are checked with synthetic inputs created for testing. Checks cover the main action, invalid input, copy or download output, language switching, and mobile layout. Network-backed tools are identified clearly and tested separately from browser-only processing.

Guide standards

Guides are based on reproducible workflows rather than copied summaries. When a claim depends on a specification, browser API, security standard, or vendor behavior, primary documentation is preferred. Examples must use invented hosts, tokens, IDs, and records rather than real customer or production data.

Updates and corrections

Pages include a reviewed or updated date. Material corrections are recorded in the public changelog. Readers can report a broken workflow, inaccurate statement, or inaccessible page through the contact page.

Advertising independence

Advertising does not determine tool output, guide conclusions, rankings, or coverage. Ads must remain distinguishable from navigation, inputs, results, copy buttons, and downloads. ToolkitBox does not accept payment to alter a technical result.

Automation and review

Automation may assist with formatting, link checks, translation consistency, and test coverage. Published pages remain the responsibility of the site maintainer and are reviewed for accuracy, usefulness, and safe examples before release.
