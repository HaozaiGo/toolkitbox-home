About

Fast developer tools that respect your workflow.

ToolkitBox is a collection of focused browser tools for formatting data, encoding text, generating identifiers, checking URLs, comparing text, inspecting SSL certificates, and working with colors.

What we build

Each tool is designed to open quickly, stay understandable, and avoid unnecessary account gates. Most processing runs locally in your browser so snippets, payloads, and test data do not need to leave your device.

How the site is organized

The main domain is the directory for developer tools. Individual tools live on subdomains such as json.toolkitbox.org, base64.toolkitbox.org, and ssl.toolkitbox.org.

Editorial approach

ToolkitBox includes practical guides that explain common developer workflows, not just links to tools. The goal is to help visitors decide when a tool is appropriate, what privacy tradeoffs to consider, and how to avoid mistakes with sensitive examples.

Maintenance and contact

The site is maintained as an independent utility directory and edited when tools, browser behavior, or privacy guidance changes. For corrections, broken links, or content questions, contact bb83100436@gmail.com.

Advertising policy

Advertising may appear on the site after review, but ads should remain visually separate from navigation, tool controls, downloads, and examples. The site does not ask visitors to click ads or treat ads as part of a tool result.

Who it is for

The site is intended for developers, testers, technical writers, students, and operators who need quick utilities for everyday data cleanup and review. It is not a replacement for professional security review, production monitoring, or legal advice.
